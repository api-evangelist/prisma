This article compares pricing between Prisma Compute (currently in public beta and free) and Vercel for hosting TypeScript applications. For a sample 20M-request workload, Prisma Compute costs approximately $98 monthly while Vercel Pro runs about $236, with the difference stemming largely from Vercel's charges for developer seats and workflow features that Prisma doesn't meter.
